THE ÉLAN SPACE
BAKED DAILY

Coffee can be a ritual, not just a routine.

ÉLAN is a small independent café built around good ingredients, thoughtful design and genuine hospitality. We wanted a place where a quick coffee could become an hour, and an ordinary morning could feel a little more special.

Our baristas work with carefully selected beans and simple techniques that let the coffee speak for itself. In the kitchen, our pastries are made in small batches so every morning starts fresh.

Whether you're opening your laptop, meeting a friend or simply taking ten quiet minutes for yourself, there's a seat waiting.
